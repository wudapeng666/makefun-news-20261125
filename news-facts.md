# News facts used in the visual

All four items below were published or materially updated on 25 November 2026 and were read from the publishers' RSS feeds at build time.

1. **Global trade:** Sky News reported that Donald Trump was set to raise tariffs on China, the EU and several other countries, affecting 16 major economies. The image uses "reports" because the feed described a planned or reported action rather than a final signed instrument.
2. **Libya / Russia / Ukraine:** France 24 reported that two Libyan soldiers died after a Ukrainian attack on Russian vessels near Benghazi. The image attributes the attack claim to Ukraine, matching the source wording.
3. **Colombia:** France 24 reported that Colombia's president said all hostages were freed and one assailant was killed after a supermarket hostage siege.
4. **Gaza:** France 24 reported that Amnesty International urged the International Criminal Court to investigate whether Israeli officials committed genocide, pointing to severe reproductive-health and birth-related harm. The group acknowledged that it could not reach a definitive conclusion; the image therefore says Amnesty urged a probe and does not present the allegation as an established verdict.
